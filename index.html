⏱ CLOCK

Tackles in bounds keep it moving. Incompletions and going out of bounds stop it. Use TIMEOUT, HURRY UP, or SPIKE when needed.

Receivers A–D run the routes shown. Throw before the break to lead them. Tight-window completions earn more points.

📱 MOBILE

Use the directional pad, A–D receiver buttons, SPRINT, and the runner skill buttons. Landscape works best.

Take the lead before time expires. Convert fourth downs, reach field-goal range, and make the final possession count.
